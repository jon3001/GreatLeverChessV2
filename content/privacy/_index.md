# Privacy

We keep this short and in plain English, because a privacy notice nobody reads
is not much use to anyone.

## Who we are

Great Lever Chess Club is a chess club that meets at [VENUE NAME AND ADDRESS].
We are responsible for the personal information described on this page.

If you have any question about your information, or you want us to change or
delete something, contact [CLUB CONTACT EMAIL] and it will reach [ROLE — e.g.
"our club secretary"].

## Visiting this website

This site does not set any cookies, and there is no cookie banner because there
is nothing to consent to.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages people
find useful. It does not store anything on your device, does not follow you to
other websites, and does not tell us who you are — we see numbers, not people.

Cloudflare also delivers this site, and like any web server it sees your IP
address as part of that. It uses this to serve the pages, to protect the site,
and to work out roughly which part of the country visits come from. We do not
see it ourselves. Cloudflare is a US company, so some of this may be processed
outside the UK, under the safeguards in its data processing terms.

Cloudflare keeps the analytics figures for six months.

## Using the contact form

If you fill in our contact form, we collect your name, your message, and
whichever of your email address and phone number you give us. This is sent
straight to [ROLE 1] and [ROLE 2], and we use it only to reply to you and to
carry on that conversation.

The form is protected from spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that you
are a person rather than a bot.

We keep enquiry emails for [12 MONTHS — CONFIRM] after our conversation ends,
then delete them. If you would rather we deleted yours sooner, just ask.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your
behalf.

## If you are a member

We hold your contact details so we can run the club — arranging teams,
letting you know about fixtures, and sorting out lifts to away matches. This
means your details may be shared with a team captain, or with another member
giving you a lift, for exactly those purposes.

We pass your details to the English Chess Federation so you can be registered
as a member and so your games can be rated. That is the reason we collect them
in the first place, and the ECF has its own privacy notice covering what it
does next.

If you join our WhatsApp group, other members in that group can see your phone
number. That is how WhatsApp works, so it is worth knowing before you join.

We keep members' contact details while you are a member and for one season
afterwards, in case you come back.

## Match results and players' names

We publish the results of matches our teams play, including the names of the
players on each board, their ratings, and the result of each game. This
information comes from the English Chess Federation's League Management System,
which publishes it too.

We are careful about how far we go with it:

- **Opposition players appear in match results only.** We do not build profile
  pages, career records or head-to-head statistics about players from other
  clubs.
- **We do not aggregate records for anyone under 18**, from any club, including
  our own.
- **Our own adult members** may have a player page showing their record for the
  club. If you would rather not, tell us and we will remove it.

If you would like us to take your name off something on this site, contact us
and we will. Please note that we can only change our own website — we cannot
remove anything from the ECF's records or the league's, and you would need to
contact them separately about that.

## Photographs

We sometimes publish photographs from club nights and matches. If you appear in
one and would rather you did not, tell us and we will take it down — no
explanation needed, and no hard feelings.

We do not name people in captions without asking them first.

If a young person under 18 is ever photographed, we ask their parent or
guardian before taking the photograph and again before publishing it.

## How long we keep things

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Contact form enquiries | [12 months — CONFIRM] after the conversation ends |
| Members' contact details | While you are a member, plus one season |
| Match results and club history | Indefinitely, as a historical record |
| Photographs | Until you ask us to remove them, or the page is retired |
| Website analytics | Six months (Cloudflare's retention period) |

## Who else sees your information

Nobody, beyond what is described above. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it
with anyone for marketing. The only organisations involved are the English
Chess Federation and the leagues we play in, which is how league chess works,
and Cloudflare, which hosts this site.

We would share information if the law required us to, or to help the police
investigate a crime. That has never happened.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy — this is
  free;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete something, or stop using it in a particular way;
- stop publishing your name or photograph on this website.

Just ask at [CLUB CONTACT EMAIL]. We will not ask you why.

## If you are unhappy

If you think we have got something wrong with your information, tell us first
at [CLUB CONTACT EMAIL]. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and
do our best to put it right.

If you are still not satisfied, you can complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/),
which is the UK regulator for this. You do not have to come to us first, but we
would rather you did.

Great Lever is an adult club. We do not run junior sessions or coaching, and we
are not able to supervise young players during club nights or matches.

We are happy to welcome players aged [16] and over, but a parent or guardian
must stay on the premises for the whole session.

We have adopted the [English Chess Federation's safeguarding
policy](https://www.englishchess.org.uk/safeguarding-and-the-ecf/). If you have
a safeguarding concern, please contact [SAFEGUARDING OFFICER NAME] at
[SAFEGUARDING EMAIL].
